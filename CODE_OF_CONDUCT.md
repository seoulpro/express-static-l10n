# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in `express-static-l10n` welcoming, safe, and
equitable for everyone, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age,
physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender
identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, religion, national or
social origin, socio-economic status, level of education, or other status.

## Expected behavior

- Engage kindly and honestly, respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback, and take responsibility for your
  actions.
- Act to repair harm and sustain the well-being of the project community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, including unwanted personal attention after a request to stop.
- Insulting, demeaning, or discriminatory comments about a person or group.
- Inappropriate sexualization of interactions, or publishing another person's
  private information without permission.
- Threats of violence, or misrepresenting your identity to evade enforcement.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or other project spaces.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project-controlled spaces — issues, pull
requests, code reviews, discussions, commit messages, and other official project
communication — and when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Conduct concerns are handled privately by the project maintainer. Report them by
email to [lim@limsumin.com](mailto:lim@limsumin.com) with a clear subject such as
`express-static-l10n conduct report`, and do not post sensitive details in a
public issue or pull request. Describe what happened, where and when, who was
involved, and any relevant links or safety needs; share only what you are
comfortable providing. Reports are handled discreetly and reviewed as promptly
as is practical.

This path is for conduct concerns only. Report security vulnerabilities through
GitHub private vulnerability reporting as described in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), not the conduct-report email.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer enforces this code of conduct and may edit, remove, or
reject contributions and interactions that violate it. Responses are
proportionate to the severity and pattern of the behavior — from a private
correction or warning to temporary restriction or permanent exclusion — and
earlier steps may be skipped when warranted.
